## 🎮 About the Game

The Adventurers is a turn-based tactical RPG developed by Eternal Vigilance Entertainment. It launched in Steam Early Access on November 11, 2024, and received its full release on August 31, 2026. Set in a high-fantasy world inspired by tabletop role-playing games, it combines party management, open-world exploration, random events, quests, inventory management, character progression, and tactical combat.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Adventurers**. This trainer enhances the campaign by modifying the resources and conditions that shape party survival. It can protect adventurers during turn-based battles, preserve health, stamina, energy, morale, and equipment, simplify item and crafting management, and provide adjustable control over game speed.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to experiment with party builds, explore the open world with fewer resource restrictions, test classes and abilities, reduce repetition, or replay the campaign with a more relaxed difficulty level.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents recruited adventurers from losing health during exploration and combat.
- **Unlimited Stamina and Energy** — Keeps the party ready for travel, abilities, events, camping, and extended adventures.
- **Maximum Morale** — Maintains high party morale when facing difficult events, dangerous journeys, and combat setbacks.
- **Unlimited Gold** — Provides a flexible campaign budget for stores, equipment, supplies, services, and other adventuring expenses.
- **Instant Crafting** — Completes crafting and equipment work without waiting through the usual in-game time.
- **Unlimited Equipment Durability** — Stops weapons and armor from losing durability during extended journeys and battles.
- **Adjustable Game Speed** — Speeds up or slows down the game for faster travel, management, or tactical encounters.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Item Quantity** — Prevents selected consumables, materials, and other inventory items from being depleted.
- **Ignore Crafting Requirements** — Allows crafting orders and equipment production without the normal material or prerequisite restrictions.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Defeat** — Greatly increases party damage so ordinary enemies can be defeated with a single successful attack.
- **No Combat Damage** — Prevents hostile attacks from reducing the health of the controlled party.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading a save and entering the world map or an active gameplay area. Adjustable game speed can make travel and routine movement faster, while unlimited stamina and energy help the party continue adventuring without frequent interruptions for rest.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the party or inventory screen before activating item-related options. Unlimited item quantity is useful for supplies, crafting materials, and consumables. Ignore crafting requirements and instant crafting are best enabled before starting a crafting order, repairing equipment, or producing upgraded gear.

### World & Environment

The campaign includes an open world with quests, random events, settlements, roads, forests, mountains, ruins, and other locations. Unlimited health, stamina, energy, morale, and gold reduce the pressure created by travel costs, event penalties, fatigue, and party upkeep while allowing broader exploration.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable combat options after an encounter begins or immediately before entering a battle. No Combat Damage protects the party during tactical encounters, while One-Hit Defeat accelerates fights against enemies and mythical creatures. Keep the trainer disabled when testing the intended balance of class abilities, positioning, terrain, morale, and party synergy.
